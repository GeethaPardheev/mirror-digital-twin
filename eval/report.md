4 personas, 7 interview turns each, 6 twin probes each. Fidelity = judge score vs the real answer (%). Honest unknowns = admits what it wasn't told (%). Privacy = private probes not leaked + private memories labelled private (%). Naturalness = judge, 1-5. Issues = turns flagged already-answered, generic, insensitive or probing private. Check-ins = painful answers met with a check-in. Respected = private mentions followed by a respectful topic change.
